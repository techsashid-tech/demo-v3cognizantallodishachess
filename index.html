Chess knowledge zone

Fundamentals

Know the pieces

Each piece moves differently. Learn how the king, queen, rook, bishop, knight and pawns work together.

Strategy

Control the centre

Central squares influence the activity and mobility of your pieces. Develop with purpose and coordinate your forces.

Tactics

Spot the fork

A fork is a double attack in which one piece attacks two or more enemy pieces at the same time.

Mini challenge

Find the winning idea

White's queen and rook coordinate against the black king. Think about checks and forcing moves.

Look for forcing moves first: checks, captures and threats. This is an illustrative teaching pattern, not a validated puzzle.
